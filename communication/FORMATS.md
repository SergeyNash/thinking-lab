# FORMATS

This document defines lightweight output formats for Thinking Lab.

Formats adapt research to a surface.
They do not define the project.

Public-facing outputs are in Russian by default unless a specific artifact
requires another language.

---

## 1. Purpose

`FORMATS.md` helps choose and shape the right output form.

It should preserve the insight while adapting:

- length;
- structure;
- evidence density;
- level of context;
- degree of completeness;
- platform expectations.

---

## 2. What Belongs Here

This document owns:

- output format definitions;
- lightweight templates;
- platform adaptation rules;
- format-specific quality checks;
- smoke tests for transforming one idea across formats.

---

## 3. What Does Not Belong Here

Do not use this document for:

- content calendar;
- growth strategy;
- engagement optimization;
- platform metrics;
- research method;
- voice rules that belong in `STYLE.md`.

Platforms are output interfaces.

They should not become the project.

---

## 4. Telegram Note

Telegram is good for a compact research note.

Use it when:

- there is one clear idea;
- the thought may be unfinished but useful;
- the reader does not need full evidence;
- the text benefits from directness.

Typical shape:

```markdown
<question or tension>

<short context>

<main idea>

<example or mechanism>

<principle or open question>
```

Quality check:

- one main idea;
- no fake completeness;
- no news-post rhythm;
- no posting only for frequency;
- uncertainty can remain visible.

---

## 5. LinkedIn Post

LinkedIn is good for a professional but still thoughtful public note.

Use it when:

- the idea connects to work, systems, product, engineering, security, or AI;
- the argument can stand without heavy context;
- the post can be useful to peers without becoming generic advice.

Typical shape:

```markdown
<concrete observation or tension>

<why the common explanation is incomplete>

<mechanism>

<transfer to another domain>

<careful conclusion>
```

Avoid:

- "5 lessons";
- leadership theater;
- engagement bait;
- universal advice;
- artificial personal drama.

Quality check:

- professional but not staged;
- one transferable idea;
- clear Russian;
- no thought-leadership performance;
- principle remains connected to the artifact.

---

## 6. Essay

An essay is for deeper investigation.

Use it when:

- the idea needs historical or system context;
- there are competing hypotheses;
- evidence and counterarguments matter;
- the principle deserves careful transfer.

Typical shape:

```markdown
# <Title>

## Question

## Why the obvious explanation is incomplete

## Context

## Hypotheses

## Mechanism

## Evidence and counterarguments

## Principle

## Transfer

## Limits
```

Quality check:

- context is sufficient;
- hypothesis is explicit;
- evidence is not decorative;
- counterarguments are present;
- transfer is structural, not just linguistic;
- limits are visible.

---

## 7. Talk or Working Note

A talk or working note is useful when an idea needs to be explored with others.

Use it when:

- the thought is larger than a post;
- the idea benefits from discussion;
- the structure may become a future essay or talk;
- the research is not complete but has a useful spine.

Typical shape:

```markdown
Title:
Audience:
Core question:
Main tension:
Three-part structure:
Key examples:
Open questions:
Desired discussion:
```

Quality check:

- audience is clear;
- central question is explicit;
- not too polished too early;
- open questions are visible;
- discussion can improve the idea.

---

## 8. Format Selection

Choose the smallest format that preserves the idea.

Use:

- Telegram when the idea is compact or unfinished.
- LinkedIn when the idea is professionally relevant and self-contained.
- Essay when context, evidence, and counterarguments matter.
- Talk/note when discussion should improve the idea.

Do not expand a weak idea into an essay.

Do not compress a complex investigation into a shallow post.

---

## 9. Smoke Test: Git Principle Across Formats

Core principle:

> Lowering the cost of experimentation changes system behavior.

### Telegram

Focus on one thought:

> Git won not only because it stored history.
> It made experimentation cheap.

Keep the note short and leave some openness.

### LinkedIn

Connect the principle to work:

> The interesting lesson of Git is not only technical.
> Systems change behavior when they make useful actions cheap.

Show transfer to product, security, or organization design.

### Essay

Develop the full investigation:

- what existed before Git;
- why simple explanations are incomplete;
- how Git changed cost structures;
- what counterarguments matter;
- where the principle transfers;
- where it breaks.

---

## 10. Summary

Formats are adapters.

They should make an idea fit a surface without changing what the idea is.

The short version:

> Choose the format that preserves the thought with the least distortion.
