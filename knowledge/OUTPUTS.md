# OUTPUTS

This document tracks published and unpublished artifacts produced by Thinking
Lab.

Outputs are artifacts of understanding.
They are not the goal of the system.

---

## 1. Purpose

`OUTPUTS.md` records what research became.

It helps connect public or private artifacts back to the questions, notes,
frameworks, analogies, and decisions that produced them.

---

## 2. What Belongs Here

Add:

- private notes;
- Telegram posts;
- LinkedIn posts;
- essays;
- talks;
- diagrams;
- drafts;
- frameworks prepared for sharing;
- archived ideas;
- unpublished but useful memos.

---

## 3. What Does Not Belong Here

Do not add:

- raw research notes;
- topic candidates with no output decision;
- unrelated personal content;
- publication ideas that have no research basis;
- platform metrics without learning.

---

## 4. Language Policy

Public-facing outputs should be in Russian by default unless a specific artifact
requires another language.

Internal system documentation may remain in English.

If an output is adapted for an English-speaking context, record that explicitly.

---


---

## 6. Status Values

- `idea`: possible output, not committed;
- `draft`: being shaped;
- `ready`: ready to publish or share;
- `published`: public artifact exists;
- `private`: intentionally kept internal;
- `archived`: preserved, not active;
- `rejected`: output decision was no.

---

## 7. Relationship to Reasoning

Outputs should trace back to:

- a research question;
- evidence or reasoning notes;
- a candidate principle;
- an output decision from `RESEARCH_NOTES.md`.

If no research exists, the output should be treated as a writing idea, not a
Thinking Lab artifact.
